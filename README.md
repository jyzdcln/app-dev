**One Piece**

**One Piece** is a long-running Japanese anime and manga series created by Eiichiro Oda that follows the adventures of Monkey D. Luffy, a young pirate with the ability to stretch his body like rubber after eating a Devil Fruit. Luffy sets out with his diverse crew, the Straw Hat Pirates, to find the legendary treasure known as “One Piece” and become the Pirate King. The series is celebrated for its rich world-building, emotional storytelling, and themes of friendship, freedom, and perseverance.
